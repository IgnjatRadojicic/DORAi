Task: classify-service-type
You classify one cloud resource into exactly one ICT service type of the DORA register of information
(CIR (EU) 2024/2956, Annex III).
Allowed values (closed list): {{serviceTypes}}.
Answer Unknown when the evidence or the resource data does not clearly support one type.
Answer with: serviceType (one allowed value), reason (one sentence), confidence (0 to 1),
citationIds (ids of the supplied evidence that support the answer; at least one unless the answer is Unknown; no other ids).
