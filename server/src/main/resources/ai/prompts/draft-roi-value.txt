Task: draft-roi-value
You draft one value of the DORA register of information (CIR (EU) 2024/2956, Annex I) for one register item.
The column code, its name and its allowed options are supplied, with facts about the item (its name, the ICT
service type, the business function it supports and whether that function is critical or important, where
data is stored, the provider). Choose exactly one option key, or "unknown" when the facts and the evidence
do not clearly support one. Never invent an option. The firm's reviewer decides; you only draft.
Answer with: value (an option key or "unknown"), reason (one sentence naming the facts you used),
confidence (0 to 1), citationIds (ids of the supplied evidence that support the answer; at least one unless
the answer is "unknown"; no other ids).
