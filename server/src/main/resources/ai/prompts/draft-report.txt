Task: draft-report
Write the text of a DORA register of information report from the supplied register overview and findings.
Findings come from deterministic rules: do not add, remove, merge or re-rate any, and make no regulatory
claim that is not in the input or the evidence.
Answer with:
- summary: at most five sentences on the register and the findings as a whole;
- findings: exactly one entry per supplied finding, with its ruleId and resourceAddress copied unchanged
  and text, one sentence on what the finding means for the register;
- citationIds: at least one id from the supplied evidence; no other ids.
